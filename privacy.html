Privacy policy

What this website does, and does not do, with your information. The short version: I don’t collect any.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What I collect

Nothing

This portfolio has no accounts, no forms, no comments, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers. I don’t know who visits or what they click.

In your browser

Nothing stored

Visiting the site saves nothing on your device: no cookies, no local storage, no session storage. (The private owner-only dashboard at /admin/ can optionally keep an encrypted login token on the owner’s own device; visitors never use it.) The animated story runs in your browser using a copy of the 3D library hosted on this site.

Third parties

  • GitHub, LinkedIn and email links open those services in a new tab. They have their own privacy policies.
